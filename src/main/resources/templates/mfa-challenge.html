Verify it's you

Enter the current six-digit code from the authenticator you enrolled. It may be on your phone or another trusted device.

On a desktop, open the authenticator on your phone and read the current code. No authenticator app needs to be installed on this computer.

Open authenticator

Open CATS Mobile — a separate app, only if you use it for codes.

Install CATS Mobile for Android

Other authenticators: open your chosen app manually. Third-party launching is best effort; if the app does not open, use the store fallback or launch your authenticator from the home screen.

If you lose your device, ask an administrator to reset MFA only after identity verification.
